Print at original scale, in millimeters. Required quantities, orientations,
materials and supports depend on the part: follow the official instructions.
A physical robot also requires motors, bearings, belts, fasteners, electronics
and hardware firmware. These STL files alone are not a complete robot.

Printing and assembly: http://thor.angel-lm.com/documentation/
Bill of materials: http://thor.angel-lm.com/documentation/bom/

Editable CAD: https://github.com/AngelLM/Thor/tree/286b081fe6f056d87c379b884781ef77ff6a0159/freecad-src

The AXIS Arduino subset and Axis.h are educational simulation interfaces,
not Thor firmware. Follow Thor's controller and firmware documentation.
